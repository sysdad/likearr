Clean up is off

An optional review of albums on disk that nothing on Spotify asks for.

Clean up finds every album with files that no Spotify like, follow or playlist backs, and lets you decide, artist by artist, what to keep and what to trash. The page only exports your decisions: moving files (prune-stage) and anything on Spotify (promote-save) stay commands you run yourself, and trash goes to a holding folder that likearr never empties.

Carrying out a Clean up needs the library mounted at Lidarr's exact path in the likearr-cli container, and a holding folder on the same filesystem, outside the library. Turn it on in Settings > Advanced.
